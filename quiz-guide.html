PREPARE · REVIEW · TAKE YOUR QUIZ

Your first quiz, step by step

Choose a topic. Review first. Start when you are ready. These screenshots use SDLC as an example; you can select any topic.

1. Choose your topic2. Read, then review the matching cards3. Enter your name and start4. Select one answer, then click Next5. Results

STEP 1

Choose your topic

On the Academy home page, find Learning focus. SDLC is selected by default. Click the dropdown to choose any other topic your instructor assigns, such as STLC or ServiceNow. The learning links, review cards and quiz follow your selection.

STEP 2

Read, then review the matching cards

Open the learning link for your topic and read the material. Return to the Academy and click A little review goes a long way. This opens the review window. Try answering each card before checking its answer; use Next to continue. Review all 25 cards, then close the window.

For SDLC, connect each lifecycle stage to its purpose and QA responsibilities. For STLC, review test planning, cases, execution and defects. For ServiceNow, follow the workflow and configuration rules stated in the material. If you cannot explain an answer, revisit the material or ask your instructor.

STEP 3

Enter your name and start

Under Ready to check your understanding?, click Student name and type the name your instructor recognizes. Check your topic once more. Click Start topic quiz → only when you are ready: this starts the timer.

STEP 4

Select one answer, then click Next

Read the question and all four choices. Click the circle beside your answer, then click Next. Check the counter and timer as you work. You cannot go back after moving to the next question.

SDLC, STLC and ServiceNow have 25 questions and a five-minute timer. Java coding quizzes allow ten minutes. When time runs out, the attempt ends automatically. Unanswered questions do not earn points.

STEP 5

Read your result

When the quiz ends, read the topic and score in your result. Recent Scores shows saved results from the last 24 hours. If saving fails, follow the on-screen message and tell your instructor; do not assume it was saved.

Return to the matching review cards and material for concepts you found difficult.

Before you click Start

  • I selected the correct topic.
  • I read the material and reviewed its cards.
  • I can explain the main concepts in my own words.
  • I have time to finish without interruptions.
